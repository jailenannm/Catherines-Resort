About the Resort

More Than a Stay: A Bagasbas Beacon of Comfort

Catherine’s Bagasbas Lighthouse Resort is located along Bagasbas Road in Daet, Camarines Norte, near Bagasbas Beach. Guests can find it on Waze under the name Bagasbas Lighthouse Hotel Resort.

A local resort directory lists guest accommodations, a swimming pool, a restaurant, and a function hall. If you’re planning a stay or a group gathering, contact the resort to confirm current availability and arrangements.

About Our Diorama Project

This website is part of our student-built IoT diorama project inspired by Catherine’s Bagasbas Lighthouse Resort. The project models the guest journey from online reservation and admin approval to RFID check-in and access to booked facilities. The Celebration Event Package in the project brings together the Event Place, Swimming Pool, and Stage Band.
